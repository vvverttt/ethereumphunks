Everything is CC0

The artwork of every DystoLabz collection on this site (QuantumPhunks, Missing Phunks, DystoPhunkz, EthsRocks, Phikings and Phunklloween) is dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. You may copy, modify, remix, distribute and use it, commercially or not, without asking permission and without paying anyone.

Owning a piece gives you the token or inscription that records it on Ethereum. It does not give you exclusive rights to the image, because nobody holds those: under CC0 the art belongs to everyone.

The same goes for everything else: the names DystoLabz and QuantumPhunks, and this site's own code and design, are CC0 too. Take it, fork it, build on it.

A creator's note

CC0 has no conditions, and this note doesn't add any. It is the standard we hold our own work to.

Punk derivatives go back to 2017, with early one-offs like Max Osiris's Graceful Punk (2019) and Bastard Gan Punks (2020). The movement took shape with Unofficial Punks, made by Eric Rhodes (@secondrealm) on February 19, 2021, a homage to CryptoPunks that started the AltPunks movement.

The Phunks came out of that movement, PhilipInternProject and CryptoPhunks, and with them Phunkism and the Phunk collective. Around them grew the collections credited in The Shell Archive. DystoLabz builds on both: Missing Phunks, DystoPhunkz, QuantumPhunks, EthsRocks, and the special editions Phikings and Phunklloween. What we've laid is the blueprint for the next era of punk derivatives: a collection that keeps growing with everything that can be added to it, open for anyone to take from and to create the next. The collections in The Shell Archive are the greatest of this era, and they set the mark for the next.

Take whatever you want: the traits, the characters, the format. All of it is open. The one rule: make it different. New combinations, new traits, better craft than what you started with, and credit where it came from.

We don't do low-effort slop. That isn't a legal rule; holding the work to a higher standard is our ethos: anti-corporate, decentralized and open-source. Remember the past, and move it forward into the future.

The sources that shaped QuantumPhunks are credited in The Shell Archive.

What this site is

This site is an interface to smart contracts, ERC-721 tokens (like QuantumPhunks) and Ethscriptions on Ethereum. Listings, bids, sales, auctions and transfers happen on-chain, signed by your own wallet. We never hold your keys, and we cannot reverse, cancel or refund a transaction once it is confirmed.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for your wallet, your keys and every transaction you sign. Check the item, the price and the network before you confirm. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

Who sets prices

New pieces usually start with us: we list them on the market, put them up in the auction house, or both, and we set those prices and reserves. Once a piece is yours, bought or airdropped, you can list it at any price you choose, and anyone can bid on anything. After that, prices are set by buyers and sellers, not by us.

Fees

A 5% royalty is paid on every sale through our market, routed on-chain to the collection's royalty receiver. Ethereum network fees (gas) are paid by you to the network, not to us. Collections listed on other markets follow those markets' fees.

No guarantees

The site and contracts are provided as they are. Software, networks and third-party services can fail, and the value of digital collectibles can go to zero. By using this site you accept all risks associated with it, including the loss of funds. The creators of QuantumPhunks are not liable for any issues or damages that may arise from its use.

No accounts, no tracking

There are no sign-ups or passwords and no advertising or tracking cookies. You connect a wallet only when you want to trade.

What is public

Wallet addresses and transactions are public on Ethereum by design. When you connect, the site reads your address to show your items, bids and activity, which anyone can also see on a block explorer.

What stays in your browser

Your preferences (language, font, theme) and some cached collection data are saved in your own browser so pages load faster. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data.

The site is hosted and loads market and blockchain data through third-party services, which, like any website's, see your IP address and the requests your browser makes.
