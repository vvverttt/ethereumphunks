QuantumPhunks is a fully on-chain collection built on Ethscriptions. All images are inscribed directly into Ethereum transaction calldata — no IPFS, no Arweave, no external storage. Ethereum is the sole source of truth.

Ownership and transfers follow native Ethereum transactions, not smart contracts. Provenance is verifiable by reading the chain directly. Free-use, public domain art — if you hold one, you own it.

The collection began from original punk combinations translated onto animal-based characters, with hundreds of hand-constructed one-of-ones. It extends beyond 10,000 through Missing Phunks (10,000–10,250) and DystoPhunkz (10,251+), featuring 1,573 unique traits across 25 types. A comprehensive 24×24 study of the punk format — moving the culture forward while honoring its roots.
